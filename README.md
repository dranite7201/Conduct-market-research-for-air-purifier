# 🌬️ Conduct Market Research for Air Purifier

> **Objective:** This project was developed as part of the CodeBasics Air Quality Challenge. By leveraging real-world data on India's Air Quality Index (AQI), public health outcomes, population projections, and vehicle statistics, this project demonstrates how to design a data-driven product and outlines a strategic go-to-market plan for a company entering the air purifier market.

## 🎯 Project Overview (Business Questions Answered)
This dashboard serves as the foundation for targeted market research, exploring:
*   **Where is the risk highest?** Identifying the most dangerous geographic hot spots based on AQI.
*   **Where is infrastructure lacking?** Highlighting states that need better air quality monitoring stations.
*   **Are green initiatives helping?** Comparing the adoption of eco-friendly vehicles against traditional polluting ones.
*   **What does the future hold?** Forecasting future risks by analyzing demographic trends and population growth through 2036.
*   **How do we enter the market?** Using these geographic and demographic insights to conduct targeted market research for air purifiers.

## 📊 Dashboard Features

### Page 1: Monthly View
Offers a comparative analysis of monthly Air Quality Index (AQI) trends. It tracks pollution patterns across different cities, differentiates between weekday and weekend exposure levels, and provides a detailed breakdown of specific pollutant types and their overall severity categories.

### Page 3: Health Outcomes View
Investigates the physical impact of air pollution by tracking total illness cases, fatalities, and case fatality rates (CFR). This page correlates local AQI levels with direct health outcomes across specific districts and maps the geographic spread of cases and state-level mortality.

Examines how population size and growth impact air quality. This page features state-level population tables and two key scatter plots: one correlating 2024 population sizes with average AQI, and another forecasting regional population growth percentages from 2025 to 2036.
